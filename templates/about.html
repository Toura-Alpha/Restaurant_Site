The Beginning

Bella Vista was founded in 2015 by Chef Marco Bellini and his wife, Isabella. After years of working in Michelin-starred restaurants across Europe, they dreamed of creating a place where exceptional cuisine met warm, welcoming hospitality.

The name "Bella Vista" (Beautiful View) reflects their vision of providing not just excellent food, but an unforgettable dining experience that pleases all the senses.

Our Philosophy

We believe that great food starts with the finest ingredients. That's why we partner exclusively with local farms and artisanal producers who share our commitment to quality and sustainability.

Every dish that leaves our kitchen is crafted with precision, passion, and the freshest seasonal ingredients. Our menu changes quarterly to reflect the best of each season.

The Experience

At Bella Vista, dining is an event. From the moment you step through our doors, you'll be transported to an elegant Italian villa where every detail has been carefully curated for your comfort.

Our attentive staff ensures that your experience exceeds expectations, whether you're celebrating a special occasion or simply enjoying a memorable dinner with loved ones.
